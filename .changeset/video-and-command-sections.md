The default template has a new **Video** section (framed, wide and split layouts) for an `.mp4` or `.webm` file, with a poster image, a caption, and a `loop` playback option that plays muted while the video is on screen and stays paused with reduced motion. YouTube and other page links are refused with a message saying why. The hero gains an optional `command`, shown with a copy button, and the terminal call to action's printed lines are now editable (`output`) instead of fixed text.
