Starter: one cookie choice for a site and its subdomains, with nothing to set up. A visitor who answers the banner on `example.com` is not asked again on `docs.example.com`, and the reverse: the choice is also kept in a `bm-consent` cookie on the site's own domain (180 days, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on https), which the browser finds the way Google's `cookie_domain: 'auto'` does. The shared cookie wins over the site's own `localStorage`, withdrawing on one site turns analytics off on the others, and a choice made before this release moves into the cookie with its date, so nobody is asked twice. Localhost and IP addresses keep their own choice. `BEARMINION_CONSENT_DOMAIN=off` at build time keeps the choice to each host (for a domain shared with someone else's sites), and a domain such as `shop.example.com` shares it only there; any other value fails the build. Say in the banner text that the choice covers all your sites (Site tab, Edit banner).

Advanced Consent Mode, for counting visitors who reject or ignore the banner. `site.consent.mode` is `basic` (the default, as before) or `advanced`, set with the new Count visits without cookies switch under Site → Cookie banner. In advanced mode the template loads the head snippet at once, after the Consent Mode defaults, so Google tags send cookieless pings until the visitor accepts; the body snippet still waits. The default banner message then says visits are counted without cookies (`CONSENT_ADVANCED_BODY`, `consentDefaults(mode)`; `consentCopy()` takes `mode` into account). The defaults script now also grants a returning visitor straight away, before any tag runs. `CONSENT_MODES` and `ConsentMode` are exported.

New in `src/lib/consent.ts`: `consentDomain()`, `consentAdvanced()` and `consentDefaultScript()` (`CONSENT_DEFAULT_SCRIPT` stays). New in `src/lib/consent-store.ts`: `readChoiceCookie`, `choiceCookie`, `sharesDomain` and `domainCandidates`. `ConsentBanner.astro` takes a `domain` prop, and `Base.astro` passes it and handles advanced mode.

Existing sites: these files belong to no section, so `npx bearminion status` does not list them. Copy `src/lib/consent.ts`, `src/lib/consent-store.ts`, `src/lib/consent-client.ts` and `src/components/organisms/ConsentBanner.astro` from the starter, and take the consent lines of `src/layouts/Base.astro` (the defaults script, the head snippet and `<ConsentBanner>`). Without the Base.astro change the banner still shares the choice on the site's own domain, but advanced mode does nothing.
